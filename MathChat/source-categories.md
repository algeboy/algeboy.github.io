## Reading this chart

Rows show the source’s primary public role in this review; horizontal position shows openness to AI use. Categories describe source context, not motive or argument quality.

The category assignments and openness scores are recorded in [openness-by-category.csv](https://github.com/algeboy/MathChat/blob/main/data/openness-by-category.csv).
